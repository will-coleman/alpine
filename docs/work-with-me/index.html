WORK WITH ME

I work with airlines, tourist boards and travel brands. One person, so one project at a time, and I say no to things I can't do properly.

Who

Who this works for

Airlines. Route launches, new cabin products, and reviews where you're confident enough to let me say what I found. I fly the route as a normal passenger and the review reads like one. That's the only version that's worth anything to either of us.

Tourist boards and regions. Particularly the ones outside the obvious circuit. The Europe project is built on the parts of a country people skip, so a region trying to pull visitors away from its own overrun capital is a natural fit.

Travel brands. Kit that goes in the bag and gets used on camera because it's genuinely in the bag. I don't do unboxings.

Audience

Who's on the other end

The YouTube audience is people who check which aircraft is on the route before they book, and who will watch fifteen minutes about a seat. They're the ones who notice when a review is bought, which is why the ones I do aren't written for me.

The Instagram audience is smaller and moves faster. It's people planning a trip in the next few months, saving posts as a shortlist. Reach there runs well ahead of the follower count, because the posts travel through saves and shares rather than the follow.

I'll send you the actual numbers — views, retention, demographics, saves — with the rate card. I'd rather you see the real figures than a headline one on a web page.

What I deliver

1 video · 3–5 weeks

Long-form YouTube

Eight to eighteen minutes, shot on the route or in the destination, cut in my own voice. Airline reviews are the format the channel is known for and the one that performs.

3–5 clips · 2 weeks

Short-form vertical

Reels and Shorts, 15 to 60 seconds, cut for sound-off viewing with the text treatment both accounts use. Usually a set of three to five from one trip rather than a single clip.

Delivered with the edit

Stills

Licensed stills from the same shoot, delivered as full-resolution files with usage agreed in writing. Cabin, aircraft exterior, and destination.
