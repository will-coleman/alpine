29 PLACES WORTH THE DETOUR

Every place from every guide in one list. Each one has the nearest town, rough coordinates and how you actually get to it — which is the part most lists leave out.

All 29

  1. mountain 34.910 N · 32.330 E

    Avakas Gorge

    Cyprus

    A limestone gorge on the Akamas peninsula that narrows to about three metres with 30m walls and a chockstone wedged overhead. You walk up the streambed. In spring there's water in it and in August there isn't.

    Getting there. Rough track from Agios Georgios — a normal hire car makes the car park but only just. Two hours in and out. Do not go in or after rain; it floods.

    Nearest town — Pegeia

  2. viewpoint 34.960 N · 34.070 E

    Cape Greco

    Cyprus

    The southeast corner of the island: white cliffs, sea caves you can swim into, and a natural rock arch at Kamara tou Koraka. It's a national forest park, so the hotel wall stops dead at the boundary and the coast carries on.

    Getting there. Cycle or walk the coastal path from Ayia Napa, about an hour. Bus 101/102 runs along the top. Sunrise is the point — this is the first place in Cyprus the sun hits.

    Nearest town — Ayia Napa

  3. coast 45.100 N · 29.200 E

    Danube Delta

    Romania

    The river arrives at the Black Sea and breaks into 5,000 square kilometres of channels, reed beds and floating islands. It holds the largest reed bed on earth and around 300 species of bird. Villages here are reachable only by boat.

    Getting there. Ferries and fast boats from Tulcea to Sulina and Sfântu Gheorghe. Book a local boatman for the small channels — the scheduled boats stay on the main arms and you'll see a fraction of it.

    Nearest town — Tulcea

  4. viewpoint 35.850 N · 14.380 E

    Dingli Cliffs

    Malta

    The highest point on the islands at 253m, straight down into the sea, with the terraced fields running right up to the edge. Filfla island sits offshore — it was used as an RAF bombing target until 1971 and you still can't land on it.

    Getting there. Bus 52 from Rabat, or twenty minutes by car from Mdina. Walk the cliff path south past the chapel for the better angles.

    Nearest town — Dingli

  5. ruin 36.050 N · 14.270 E

    Ġgantija

    Malta

    Two megalithic temples on Gozo that went up around 3600 BC — older than Stonehenge, older than the pyramids, and built from blocks up to five metres across. The name means giantess, because nobody could work out how else they got there.

    Getting there. Ferry from Ċirkewwa to Mġarr, then the 322 bus or fifteen minutes by car. Combined ticket covers the site and the windmill next door.

    Nearest town — Xagħra, Gozo

  6. viewpoint 58.370 N · 22.670 E

    Kaali crater

    Estonia

    A meteorite hit Saaremaa around 1500 BC and left a 110m circular lake in what is now a field. It happened while people were living here, which is probably why it turns up in Baltic and Finnish mythology as a sun falling out of the sky.

    Getting there. Ferry from Virtsu to Kuivastu, then about an hour's drive. It is signposted and free, and takes twenty minutes to walk round.

    Nearest town — Kuressaare, Saaremaa

  7. viewpoint 45.610 N · 18.850 E

    Kopački Rit

    Croatia

    Floodplain where the Drava meets the Danube, right against the Serbian and Hungarian borders — one of the largest intact wetlands in Europe. White-tailed eagles, black storks, and a boardwalk that goes out into standing water.

    Getting there. Twenty minutes north of Osijek by car. Boat trips run from the visitor centre when levels allow; spring flood is the good time and also the time the boardwalk closes.

    Nearest town — Osijek

  8. viewpoint 58.920 N · 22.200 E

    Kõpu Lighthouse

    Estonia

    One of the oldest working lighthouses in the world, up since 1531, a white stone tower standing on the highest hill on Hiiumaa. You climb the inside on a narrow stair and come out at 102m above the sea with nothing but forest in every direction.

    Getting there. Ferry from Rohuküla to Heltermaa, 90 minutes, cars carried. Then 50km across the island. Book the ferry ahead in July.

    Nearest town — Kõrgessaare, Hiiumaa

  9. coast 59.550 N · 25.850 E

    Lahemaa National Park

    Estonia

    An hour east of Tallinn and immediately empty: four peninsulas of pine forest running into the Baltic, bog boardwalks, glacial boulders the size of vans, and three restored manor houses. Käsmu was a smugglers' village and looks it.

    Getting there. Buses from Tallinn to Võsu take about 90 minutes but leave you stranded between trailheads. Hire a car for the day — the loop is easy driving.

    Nearest town — Võsu

  10. coast 34.940 N · 32.310 E

    Lara Bay

    Cyprus

    A protected sand bay on the west of the Akamas where loggerhead and green turtles nest between June and September. There are no sunbeds, no bar and no road worth the name, which is the arrangement that keeps the turtles.

    Getting there. Dirt track north from Agios Georgios, 40 minutes, and a 4x4 is genuinely the right vehicle. Nesting areas are fenced and stay closed at night.

    Nearest town — Pegeia

  11. coast 42.760 N · 16.900 E

    Lastovo

    Croatia

    A closed military island until 1989, so it missed thirty years of tourism entirely. Forty-six islands and islets in the group, one proper village of stone houses with chimneys built like small towers, and some of the darkest night sky in the Adriatic.

    Getting there. Ferry from Split via Hvar and Korčula — four and a half hours, once or twice a day. Bring what you need; the shops are small and the last boat back is early.

    Nearest town — Ubli

  12. food 35.840 N · 14.540 E

    Marsaxlokk

    Malta

    The fishing harbour on the southeast corner, full of luzzu boats painted with eyes on the bow. Sunday morning is the fish market. The restaurants along the front are the reason to come on any other day — order whatever came in that morning.

    Getting there. Bus 81 or 85 from Valletta, about 40 minutes. Sunday gets busy by ten.

    Nearest town — Marsaxlokk

  13. town 35.890 N · 14.400 E

    Mdina

    Malta

    The old capital, walled, on a hill in the middle of the island, with about 250 residents and almost no cars. Day trips leave around six and the streets go completely quiet — that's the hour worth being there for.

    Getting there. Bus 51, 52 or 53 from Valletta, half an hour. Enter at the main gate and leave through the Greeks' Gate on the far side.

    Nearest town — Rabat

  14. town 40.040 N · 7.110 W

    Monsanto

    Portugal

    A village built in and around granite boulders the size of houses — roofs wedged under them, walls running into them, one boulder serving as somebody's ceiling. There's a ruined castle at the top and a very long view into Spain.

    Getting there. About 50 minutes from Castelo Branco by car. The village road is single track and steep; leave the car in the lower car park.

    Nearest town — Castelo Branco

  15. ruin 34.960 N · 33.030 E

    Panagia tou Araka

    Cyprus

    One of ten painted churches in the Troodos on the UNESCO list. From outside it's a barn with a steep timber roof; inside it's a complete cycle of twelfth-century Byzantine frescoes, unrestored, in the original colours.

    Getting there. In Lagoudera, an hour of mountain road from Nicosia. The key is held locally and the priest opens it — turning up mid-morning is the reliable approach.

    Nearest town — Kakopetria

  16. town 34.870 N · 33.310 E

    Pano Lefkara

    Cyprus

    A hill village of silver workshops and lace, the trade going back far enough that Leonardo is supposed to have bought an altar cloth here in 1481. The old streets behind the main strip are where people actually live.

    Getting there. Forty minutes from Larnaca on the A1 then up. Park at the entrance; the lanes are too narrow. Weekday mornings are quiet.

    Nearest town — Larnaca

  17. mountain 41.730 N · 8.150 W

    Peneda-Gerês

    Portugal

    The only national park in the country, up against the Spanish border. Granite, wild ponies, and villages with communal grain stores still standing on stilts. The Arado waterfall is the one everyone finds; walk twenty minutes upstream and you get it to yourself.

    Getting there. Two hours from Porto by car. Buses reach Arcos de Valdevez and then stop being useful — you need your own wheels for the park roads.

    Nearest town — Arcos de Valdevez

  18. town 40.230 N · 7.830 W

    Piódão

    Portugal

    A schist village stacked up one side of a valley in the Serra do Açor, every house the same dark slate, every door and window frame painted blue. It was cut off by road until the 1970s, which is why it still looks like this.

    Getting there. Ninety minutes from Coimbra on mountain roads that take longer than the map says. No bus worth the name. Park at the top and walk down.

    Nearest town — Arganil

  19. coast 38.790 N · 9.480 W

    Praia da Ursa

    Portugal

    Sea stacks and black sand under the cliffs just north of Cabo da Roca, the westernmost point of mainland Europe. The descent is a steep unmade path with a rope in one section, which is the only reason it isn't rammed.

    Getting there. Drive to Cabo da Roca from Sintra, then the unmarked pull-in about 1km north. Twenty minutes down, thirty-five back up. Not at high tide, and not in the dark.

    Nearest town — Colares

  20. town 45.110 N · 15.580 E

    Rastoke

    Croatia

    Where the Slunjčica drops into the Korana in a set of waterfalls, with a village built directly on top of them — mill houses straddling the channels, water running under the floors. It is what Plitvice looks like with people living in it.

    Getting there. Fifteen minutes from Plitvice's northern entrance on the D1, so it works as the stop on the way out. Small paid entry to the mill village.

    Nearest town — Slunj

  21. mountain 45.370 N · 22.880 E

    Retezat

    Romania

    Around eighty glacial lakes in one range, including the deepest and the largest in the country. It was Romania's first national park, declared in 1935, and the core reserve is still closed to casual walkers. Chamois and brown bear are both here in numbers.

    Getting there. Car to Cârnic or Gura Zlata, then walk in — there is no road through. Bukura Lake is six hours from the trailhead. Register at the park office.

    Nearest town — Hațeg

  22. town 46.450 N · 23.570 E

    Rimetea

    Romania

    A Hungarian village under the limestone wall of Piatra Secuiului, every house whitewashed with green shutters and two windows facing the street. The sun comes up twice here, in a manner of speaking — it clears the ridge, drops behind it and comes back.

    Getting there. About an hour from Cluj by car via Turda. The path up the mountain behind the village takes two hours and starts from the main square.

    Nearest town — Turda

  23. ruin 59.220 N · 24.200 E

    Rummu quarry

    Estonia

    A Soviet limestone quarry worked by prison labour until 1991, then abandoned and left to flood. The water is very clear, the spoil heap next to it is white, and parts of the prison buildings are still under the surface.

    Getting there. 45 minutes west of Tallinn by car. It's a paid, managed site now with proper access — the free-for-all trespassing era ended and the fences are real.

    Nearest town — Rummu

  24. town 46.220 N · 24.790 E

    Sighișoara

    Romania

    A walled Saxon citadel still lived in, with nine towers left of the original fourteen, each built and paid for by a different guild. Vlad III was born in a house on the main square, which the town has never quite stopped mentioning.

    Getting there. On the main railway line — direct trains from Brașov (2h) and Cluj (3h). Walk up the covered wooden staircase to the church on the hill.

    Nearest town — Sighișoara

  25. viewpoint 58.440 N · 25.050 E

    Soomaa

    Estonia

    Raised bog and floodplain forest where the spring melt regularly puts the roads and gardens under a metre of water. Estonians call it the fifth season and deal with it by getting the canoe out. The rest of the year it's boardwalks over open bog.

    Getting there. An hour from Pärnu or Viljandi by car. Flood season is usually late March to April and local operators run canoe trips through the trees.

    Nearest town — Viljandi

  26. mountain 45.600 N · 24.620 E

    Transfăgărășan

    Romania

    90km of hairpins over the Făgăraș mountains, built in the 1970s on Ceaușescu's orders after Soviet tanks rolled into Czechoslovakia and he wanted a military road across the range. It tops out at 2,042m at Bâlea Lake.

    Getting there. Drive it north to south for the better corners. It is shut by snow from roughly November to the end of June — check before you commit to a route. Allow half a day, not two hours.

    Nearest town — Cârțișoara

  27. coast 43.050 N · 16.190 E

    Vis and Komiža

    Croatia

    The furthest inhabited island off the coast, and shut to foreigners until 1989 for the same reason as Lastovo. Komiža on the west side is a fishing town with a Venetian tower in the harbour, and Stiniva cove is round the corner through a gap in the cliff.

    Getting there. Ferry from Split, two hours twenty, or the fast catamaran in half that with no cars. Stiniva is a steep twenty-minute scramble down, or a five-minute boat ride from Komiža.

    Nearest town — Vis

  28. coast 36.070 N · 14.230 E

    Wied il-Għasri

    Malta

    A narrow inlet cut into the north Gozo cliffs, ending in a pebble beach about ten metres wide with vertical rock either side. Snorkelling here is the best on the islands. In any swell it's dangerous and it closes itself.

    Getting there. Park at the top of the valley road near Għasri and take the stone steps down. There is no bus stop and no shade at the bottom.

    Nearest town — Għasri, Gozo

  29. mountain 45.750 N · 15.350 E

    Žumberak

    Croatia

    A limestone range on the Slovenian border, 45 minutes from Zagreb and almost entirely ignored. Beech forest, vineyards on the south slopes, Greek Catholic churches, and marked walking that connects villages of four or five houses.

    Getting there. Car from Zagreb via Samobor. Trails start from Sošice and Budinjak. There is one bus a day and it is not enough.

    Nearest town — Samobor
